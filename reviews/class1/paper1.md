## Errors (must fix)

| Q | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 43 | The chart shows all 12 months of one year, with only Apr, Jul and Oct starred. If "the gap is always the same" (3 months), January on the same chart should also be starred, so the figure contradicts the keyed answer (January). "Just before April" can also be read literally as March (option B). | Ask forward instead: "A water tank is cleaned in the starred months on the chart. The gap between cleanings is always the same. After October, in which month will the tank be cleaned next?" Options: (A) December (B) November (C) February (D) January. Answer D. Solution: "April to July is 3 months, and July to October is 3 months. 3 months after October is January." |
| Cover | The parent note says "The answer sheet and the answer key … Detach them before the child begins", but the child's instructions say "mark your answer on the answer sheet at the end". The child needs the answer sheet. | "The answer sheet is at the end; give it to the child. Detach the answer key and solutions before the child begins." |
| Cover | "All figures are not necessarily drawn to scale unless stated." The phrase is logically wrong ("All … are not" means none are) and "unless stated" is redundant. It is also meaningless or confusing for a 6-year-old in a paper where pictures are meant to be counted. | Remove it, or write: "Pictures may not be drawn to scale." |

## Level / syllabus / difficulty

- All content fits Class 1: numbers to 100, place value, +/− within 20, basic shapes, coins and notes, days and months, and patterns.
- Q15 (6+6+6, repeated addition) is labelled Easy. Medium fits better for Class 1.
- Q42 combines a repeating pattern with a mirror fold, which is a two-step reasoning question. It is fair for a star question, but only if the wording is clarified as above.
- Q43 is hard but fair once fixed. Q41 (+9 pattern via digits), Q44 (count days, then tens plus ones) and Q45 (mirror spots) are good star questions.
- Q32 is very easy because the coins are labelled with their values. That is acceptable as an Easy question.
- Distractors are generally good: reversals (81, 36, 61), off-by-one answers (69, 72, 13/14) and the "wrong operation" answer (23 in Q20). Q33 "Sunday" and Q16 "2" are weak but harmless.
