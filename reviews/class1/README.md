## Must fix (2 broken questions plus solution errors)

1. **Paper 1 Q43 (water tank, months).** The chart shows one whole year with only April, July and October starred. January is *not* starred, yet January is the keyed answer, so the picture contradicts the answer. "Just before April" can also be read as March (option B).
   Rewrite: *"A water tank is cleaned in the starred months on the chart. The gap between cleanings is always the same. After October, in which month will the tank be cleaned next?"* The answer stays (D) January.
2. **Paper 4 Q14 (plates of cherries).** Each cherry picture shows **two** cherries on one stem. A child counting single cherries gets 10, 10 and 6, which is already more than 5 per plate, so the question has no correct answer. Use a single-fruit picture (for example one apple) with 5, 5 and 3 on the plates.
3. **Paper 3 solutions.** The Q31 solution says "Our coins are ₹1, ₹2, ₹5 and ₹10". A ₹20 coin also exists, and ₹20 is one of the options. The Q28 solution says "each side is 2 dots long", but each side covers 3 dots (2 spaces).
4. **Paper 5 Q31** (in the question itself): "Our coins are ₹1, ₹2, ₹5 and ₹10" is factually incomplete for the same reason. Drop that sentence; the options already list the coins.
5. **Paper 2 key Q45:** the answer cell shows only "(C)", and the wrong-option stick counts are listed out of order. **Paper 1 key Q34:** the answer cell shows only "(B)".

## Issues that repeat across all 5 papers (fix once in the template)

### Cover page
- **"All figures are not necessarily drawn to scale unless stated."** The logic is wrong ("All … are not" means *none* are), "unless stated" is never used in the papers, and the line means nothing to a 6-year-old. It also clashes with questions where children must judge a shape by eye (squares, equal halves). **Remove it from the child's instructions.** If you want to keep it, put "Pictures may not be drawn to scale." in the parents' box.
- **"Look at the picture or read the question carefully."** This should be "and". Rewrite: *"Read each question and look at its picture carefully. Then mark your answer on the answer sheet."*
- **"The answer sheet and the answer key … Detach them before the child begins."** This is unclear, because the child needs the answer sheet. Rewrite: *"Give the child the answer sheet. Remove the answer key and solutions before the child begins."*
- *(Optional)* The score line "Section I correct: ___ / 40 × 2" looks like a fraction. Use "___ correct × 2 = ___ marks".

## Repeats across the 5 papers

None of the 225 questions is copied word for word, but some question types come up much more often than others:

- **Bundles of 10 and loose sticks or objects:** 9 questions (P1 Q11; P2 Q2, Q8; P3 Q11, Q41; P4 Q3, Q7, Q8; P5 Q6). This is too many; swap 3 or 4 of them for number-line or before/after/between questions.
- **Swapping the two digits:** P2 Q9, P3 Q10, P4 Q10.
- **Fact families:** P1 Q20 (7 + 8 = 15, so 15 − 8) and P2 Q19 (9 + 5 = 14, so 14 − 5) are almost the same. P4 Q16 is similar.
- **Counting back by 2s:** P2 Q39 and P5 Q38.
- **Days and months:** every paper uses Q29–Q34 on day after/before/between and months in order. "Which months are in order" or "which month is missing" appears in P1 Q34, P2 Q30, P2 Q33, P4 Q30, P4 Q34 and P5 Q30. Some variety would help, for example "today is Monday, what day is it 2 days later?" or "which month has the festival…".
- **Symmetry / folding:** 8 questions (P1 Q25, Q42, Q45; P2 Q24; P3 Q26; P4 Q26, Q28; P5 Q27). That is reasonable, but note that 3 of them are in Paper 1.
- **Star questions follow the same layout in every paper:** Q41 is a number pattern, Q42 a shape pattern, Q43 a months or money pattern, Q44 a notes-and-coins total, and Q45 a shape count. This is fine for practice, but children who do all 5 papers will learn the layout. Consider mixing up the order.
