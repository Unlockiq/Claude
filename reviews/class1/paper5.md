## Language & clarity

| Q | Current wording | Issue | Suggested rewrite |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q17 | "7 planes are at the airport. 5 more planes land. …" | Sentence begins with a numeral. | "There are 7 planes at the airport. Then 5 more planes land. How many planes are at the airport now?" |
| Q18 | "A tea stall has 15 clean cups. 6 cups get used. …" | The second sentence begins with a numeral. | "A tea stall has 15 clean cups. People use 6 of the cups. How many clean cups are left?" |
| Q19 | "18 children are on the playground swings and slides. Some go home, and 11 children are still playing. …" | Begins with a numeral. | Q12 | "How many numbers are greater than 37 and less than 42?" | Two comparisons in one sentence are hard for Class 1. | "How many numbers come between 37 and 42?" |
| Q21 | "One more plate with the same number of slices is added. How many slices are there then?" | Passive voice. "there then" is awkward. | "Look at the plates of watermelon slices. Mum puts one more plate with the same number of slices on the table. How many slices are there now?" |
| Q27 | "In which picture are the two sides of the dotted line NOT the same?" | "Two sides of the dotted line" is vague. A child may think of the line's own sides. | "Look at the four shapes. Each has a dotted line. In which picture does the dotted line NOT cut the shape into two same halves?" |

| Q33 | "Myra writes down the rainfall every month…" | "Rainfall" (recording it) is an unfamiliar idea for age 6. The maths is fine. | (Optional) "Myra plants one seed every month from June to September. For how many months does she plant a seed?" |
| Q24 | "I am round. I have no corners at all." | "Round" gives the answer away; it is the only round option. This is acceptable for an Easy question but trivial. | "I have no corners and no straight sides. Which shape am I?" |
