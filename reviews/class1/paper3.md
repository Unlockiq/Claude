## Level / syllabus / difficulty

- Everything is within the Class 1 syllabus (numbers to 99, add/subtract within 20, place value, 2D shapes, coins/notes, days/months, patterns).
- Q32 uses ₹200 and ₹500 notes. These are larger denominations than Class 1 money work usually covers, though the task is only recognising a fake ₹30 note, so it is acceptable. The solution's note list leaves out ₹1, ₹2, ₹5 and ₹2000, which are still legal tender. Better: "We have notes of ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹200 and ₹500, but there is no ₹30 note."
- Q16 ("groups of 3") and Q19 (the equals sign as a balance) are pre-multiplication and pre-algebra ideas. Both are fine as Easy/Medium.
- Q23 is very easy (circle hole, circle piece); Easy is right. Q40 is labelled Medium but is about Easy.
- Star questions: Q41 (decreasing bundles, step −8) and Q42 (triangular stack) are suitably hard and fair. Q43 (count on in tens across months) and Q44 (₹42 − ₹30) are closer to Medium-hard. They are acceptable, but the star section is on the gentle side.
- Weak distractors: Q24 option (A) 0; Q3 options 41 and 45 (not multiples of ten, easy to rule out); Q10 option (C) 20. They are harmless, but replacing them with plausible slips would make the questions stronger (e.g. Q3 use 55; Q24 use 5).
