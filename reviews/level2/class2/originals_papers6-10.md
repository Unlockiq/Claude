### Broken or ambiguous questions
- **P7 Q29 (cat made of triangles): broken.** Counting strictly gives 11: ears 2+2, the blue and green inverted triangles, yellow, red, tail tip 2, purple tail. The head is a trapezoid because its sides end on the blue line, and the body's cyan/green sides never meet. 11 is not an option. The key says C (13) while its own solution says 14.
- **P7 Q24 (odd one out: 4+3+1, 2+3+4, 3+4+5, 1+2+3): two answers.** The key picks A ("not consecutive"), but B is the only odd total (9).
- **P9 Q24 (3+3+3, 2×2, 4+4+4+4, 8+4): three defensible answers.** The key picks D because the others are perfect squares, which is not Class 2 content. B is the only multiplication, and A is the only odd result.
- P6 Q24 (1+2=3, 2+3=5, 2+1=3, 3+0=3): weak ambiguity. B is the only total of 5, but D is the only one with 0.
- P8 Q17 (bananas): the 4th banana mostly hides behind the others, so 3 is a fair reading. The key gives 4.
- P10 Q29 (rectangles): 16 counts the purple square as a rectangle; a child who leaves it out gets 15, which is not an option. Separately, the embedded image prints "RECTANGLES: 16 | CIRCLES: 2 | TRIANGLES: 2". The page crop hides this banner, but anyone who extracts the image gets the answer.
- P9 Q13 (clock 8:50): the hour hand is drawn almost exactly on 9, which pulls children towards 9:50 (option A). The time is also out of syllabus.

### Wrong or mismatched solution texts (16)
- **The number contradicts the key:** P6 Q29 says 8 (key 7). P7 Q29 says 14 (key 13).
- **The number is wrong:** P6 Q22 says 12. P8 Q22 says 9. P8 Q29 says 7. P9 Q29 says 12.
- **The wording does not match the picture:**
  - P7 Q17 says "Row 1: 3, Row 2: 1", but the picture is one row of 4.
  - P7 Q21 says "Row 1: 5, Row 2: 2", but the picture is one row of 7.
  - P8 Q21 says "3 + 1", but the picture is 2 + 2.
  - P10 Q21 says "Row 1: 3, Row 2: 1" and "skip pencils/rulers", but the picture is scattered and shows caps, pens and stones.
  - P6 Q17's hint says "skip tulips and sunflowers"; there are none in the picture.
  - P10 Q16/Q17 and P8 Q16 use "Row 1 / Row 2" for scattered layouts.
- **The text is about the wrong shape:** P10 Q29 talks about triangles in a rectangles question.
- **Out-of-level methods:** P7 Q11 uses "2.67/8". P9 Q11 uses 16/40 and 15/40. P10 Q11 uses decimals 0.75 and 0.67.
- **Muddled reasoning:** P8 Q1 ("525? But 5 is used").
- **Garbled tick marks:** P9 Q8 has stray ✓ characters.

The model solutions are boilerplate. Every figure-count question reads "Counting all … gives N" or "Row 1: x, Row 2: y" without looking at the figure.
