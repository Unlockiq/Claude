# AMO L2 Extra Mock, Class 7: review of the original Tests 06–10

Source: `c7/clean/AMO_L2_Extra_Mock_Class7_Test{06..10}_{QP,AK}.pdf`. Each QP has 10 pages and 30 MCQs. Each AK has 4 pages with an answer, a hint and a solution for every question.
Method: I rendered every page at 110 dpi and read the text with pdftotext. I solved every question before opening its key, and I looked closely at each figure (Q6 concentric circles, Q13 tray, Q20 frame).

**Does each AK belong to its QP?** Yes, for all five. Every QP is headed "Practice Paper N" and its AK is headed "Practice Paper N". The key's solutions use the same numbers as the questions in the QP, and all 150 keyed answers match the questions as printed.

Verdict codes: OK / WRONG KEY / NO CORRECT OPTION / TWO ANSWERS / FIGURE PROBLEM. "FLAWED" marks a question whose premise is impossible even though the keyed option matches the intended working.
Syllabus codes: CI = compound/simple interest (C8+). CIRC = circle area or circumference outside cylinder work. ROOT = square/cube roots or perfect squares/cubes (C8). ID = algebraic identities (C8). QUAD = quadratic. SIM = simultaneous equations. OFF-LIST = topic not in the C7 list but not banned (clock, speed/time, work/pipes, HCF/LCM). Every paper uses "Rs." throughout and never ₹. The Rs. counts below cover QP text only.

## Template reuse

- Q1: linear equation from a perimeter
- Q2: remainders or HCF/LCM
- Q3: full/partial container weight
- Q4: path area
- Q5: compound interest
- Q6: concentric circles
- Q7: perfect square/cube

Near-identical repeats:
- Q9: "what sum amounts to … in 2 years at 10%" appears in T07, T09 and T10 (6050, 7260 and 4840, each ÷ 1.21).
- Q11: every worker-cycle total is an exact multiple of the cycle length, so the slot never needs partial-cycle reasoning.
- The same concentric-circle and frame figures are reused in every paper.

Because the skeleton is shared, any slot-level syllabus problem appears in every paper. The CI, circle and identity slots alone (Q5, Q6, Q8, Q9 and Q30) put 5 off-syllabus questions into each paper.
