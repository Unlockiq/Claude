# Review: original AMO L2 "Extra Mock" Class 10 papers, Tests 06–10

Method: I rendered every QP page at 110 dpi and extracted the text with pdftotext. I solved all 150 questions on my own, then compared each answer with the AK.
**Figures:** none of the five QPs has a figure. `pdfimages` finds no embedded images, and the pages hold only text, so no figure or colour checks apply. The A/B/C/D option letters are coloured, but no question depends on colour.
**Names:** Krishna (P6), Yash (P8) and Avani (P10) are all Indian names; no non-Indian names appear.
**Rs.:** every paper uses "Rs." instead of ₹, always in Q8 (the donor question) and also in Q20 of P6–P8.

Difficulty: E = 1–2 routine steps, M = a standard multi-step method (setting up a quadratic, counting with multiplicities), H = 3+ steps or a genuine insight.

- **Broken questions: 4**

  - P7 Q22: NO CORRECT OPTION. The key's arithmetic error (√741 ≠ 21) means the true answer, ≈7.22 cm, is not offered.
  - P8 Q26: TWO ANSWERS (2/7 and 4/14).
  - P9 Q9: NO CORRECT OPTION. The 13 cm off-cut is longer than the 12 cm piece.
- **Minor wording issues:** "closed cylinder" in P8 Q29 and P10 Q29. The Q18 options "No solution" and "Inconsistent" are synonyms in P7, P8 and P10, and P9 has the meaningless "Inconsistent at one point". P6 Q4 says "sweet sweets".
- **Figure problems:** 0, because the papers contain no figures.

## Syllabus violations by type

- **Hard violations of the C11 list** (complex numbers, line equations, compound-angle trig, P&C formulas, binomial theorem, variance, inequalities): **none.**
- **Borderline:** the Q27 trailing-zeros question in every paper (5) uses n! notation, which is formally introduced with C11 P&C. The counting itself is FTA/Legendre and fine; I'd accept it with a one-line definition of n!.
- **Ceilings:** all respected. Every discriminant is a perfect square with integer roots, trig uses only standard angles (30°, 45°, Pythagorean-triple cos φ), there is no compound interest, and all coordinates are integers.
- **Currency:** "Rs." appears 18 times across 10 questions (P6 Q8, Q20; P7 Q8, Q20; P8 Q8, Q20; P9 Q8; P10 Q8). All must become ₹.
- **Names / colour / figure–text conflicts:** none.
- **Coverage gaps** (not violations): the papers have no compound interest, GST, EMI, SIP, partnership, break-even, grouped-data mean/median/mode, ogive, zeroes–coefficients, division algorithm, Euclid/irrationality, BPT, frustum, pigeonhole or invariants questions. The olympiad strand (combinatorics, extremal, invariants) is nearly absent: only P9 Q7 and the Q27 trailing-zeros items.
