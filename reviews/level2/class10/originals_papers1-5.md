## Syllabus-violation summary (AMO v5.9 C10)
- **Hard violations (C11-only content):** none. There are no complex numbers, slope/line equations, compound angles, nCr/nPr, binomial theorem, variance, induction, calculus or inequalities. All trig uses standard angles or ratios read from a Pythagorean triple. Coordinates are integers, quadratics have integer roots, and there is no compound interest at all.
- **Borderline:** Q27 in every paper (5 items) asks for trailing zeros of n!. Factorial notation belongs to the C11 permutations-and-combinations chapter. It is defensible as number theory or FTA, but if the rule is strict, either define n! in the stem or replace the item.
- **Below level or off-list (not violations):** Q28 in every paper is a milk-and-water mixture (Class 7–8 ratio work). Q12 (cuboid space diagonal) and Q7 (inscribed figures) are pre-Class-10 mensuration.
- **Coverage gaps against the C10 list:** no compound interest, GST, EMI, SIP or partnership; no grouped-data mean/median/mode or ogive; no BPT; no tangent-length theorems apart from the chord-tangent item; no division algorithm or zero–coefficient relations (Q25 only evaluates P(a)); no 3-variable systems; no discriminant-nature items; no pigeonhole, extremal or invariant problems.
- **Formatting:** "Rs." appears 19 times and "₹" never does. All names (Ravi, Anika, Vihaan, Pari, Saanvi) are Indian. There are no figures, so no figure is colour-dependent or contradicts the text.

## Shared template?
**Yes. All five papers come from one 30-slot template.** Every slot has the same question type in every paper, with only the numbers and context changed: Q1 pipe and outlet leading to a quadratic, Q2 terminating decimal, Q3 fourth vertex of a parallelogram, … Q27 trailing zeros of n!, Q28 mixture, Q29 combined-solid TSA, Q30 AP sum. Many stems are word-for-word templates ("A donor wishes to share Rs. …", "The Y-axis divides the line segment…", "A polynomial P(x) … subtracting a constant k"). The consequences are:
- The five papers test exactly the same 30 skills. Students will recognise the patterns after one paper.
- The answer letters are heavily skewed. Across 150 items: A 22, B 71, C 53, **D 4**. D is the key only in P1 (Q8, Q11, Q28) and P3 (Q2). Some slots always have the same letter: Q3 always A; Q12, Q17 and Q24 always C, B and B.
- The level is CBSE-board or NCERT-exemplar, not olympiad Level 2. There are no H items, and about 72% of items are one-step E.
