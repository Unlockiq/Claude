### Difficulty
About 20 E, 10 M and 0 H per paper. The Ms are Q1 (find T, then add), Q2/Q23 figure counts, Q3 (convert and subtract), Q5, Q15 (subtract, then match an expression), Q16, Q24, Q25 and Q29 (multiply, then match "100 − x"). No question needs real insight, and the distractors are mostly arbitrary (e.g. "90 hours", "Cannot find"). These are easy for Level 2.

**Yes.** All five papers use the same 30-slot template, with only numbers, letters and figures changed:

Q1 +111 series P+T, Q2 rectangle count, Q3 length comparison with units, Q4 circles+semicircles parity, Q5 smallest even/largest odd 4-digit, Q6 3-digit subtraction, Q7 total − part length, Q8 elapsed time, Q9 letter-pair count, Q10 two parcel weights, Q11 circle count, Q12 a×b, Q13 Rs. three-amount sum, Q14 triforce triangles, Q15 "rest" as a+b expression, Q16 ages after n years, Q17 symmetry, Q18 incorrect odd/even statement, Q19 like-fraction add, Q20 letter mirror image, Q21 alternating 111-multiples series, Q22 odd shape out, Q23 figure count, Q24 half-kg cost, Q25 fraction compare, Q26 Roman sum compare, Q27 clock difference, Q28 polygon sides, Q29 pages as "100 − x", Q30 number line.
