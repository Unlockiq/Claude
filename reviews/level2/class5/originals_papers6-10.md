**Q26 in detail:** The key counts a regular side-3 triangular lattice: 6 up + 3 down + 3 medium + 1 large = 13. The figure is drawn with a taller bottom row: the two horizontal lines are at about 30% and 59% of the height, not at 1/3 and 2/3. Because of this, the two pairs of slanted lines in the bottom row cross *above* the base (at about 80% of the height) instead of meeting on it. Each crossing forms an extra small upward triangle standing on the base. Counted by line triples, the figure has 12 upward and 3 downward triangles, so 15 in total, and 15 is option D. To fix the question, redraw the figure as a regular lattice (the key's 13 then holds) or change the key to D.

### One template?

The out-of-syllabus slots (Q4, Q6, Q12, Q15, Q17, Q22, and usually Q11 and Q18) are built into the template. To fix this systematically, replace those slots rather than editing questions one at a time.
