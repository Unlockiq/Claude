# Review of the original AMO L2 Class 5 Extra Mock papers, Tests 01–05

Source: `scratchpad/c5/clean/AMO_L2_Extra_Mock_Class5_Test0{1..5}_{QP,AK}.pdf`. Each paper has 10 pages and 30 MCQs, and each key has 4 pages. The papers are titled "Practice Paper N" inside.
Method: I rendered each paper at 110 dpi, used pdftotext as well, and zoomed figures to 300 dpi where needed. I solved every question before opening its key, then compared my answer with the key.

Legend: **Syllabus**: OUT = outside the Class 5 syllabus (AMO v5.9). BORDER = not listed in the syllabus or stretches it (a Class 6 style technique), but a strong Class 5 child can do it. "Rs." = the paper uses "Rs." where it should use ₹.
Difficulty: E = easy, M = medium, H = hard (3 or more steps, or real insight needed).

### Template
Yes. All five papers come from one fixed 30-slot template. The topic in each slot is the same in every paper; only the numbers and context change:

| Slot | Topic | Slot | Topic | Slot | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | fractions | Q11 | percent | Q21 | triangle/polygon angles and names |
| Q2 | Roman numerals | Q12 | profit & loss / discount | Q22 | ratio |
| Q3 | clock/time | Q13 | BODMAS | Q23 | capacity/units |
| Q4 | interest | Q14 | averages | Q24 | pipes/speed |
| Q5 | time/fraction | Q15 | integers | Q25 | HCF/LCM |
| Q6 | algebra | Q16 | rounding/estimation | Q26 | figure counting |
| Q7 | averages | Q17 | ratio/proportion | Q27 | speed |
| Q8 | directions | Q18 | unitary method | Q28 | area/volume |
| Q9 | decimals/weights | Q19 | number series | Q29 | calendar/clock |
| Q10 | capacity/figure mensuration | Q20 | arithmetic progression | Q30 | true/false statements |

Because of this, the same violations repeat in the same slots in every paper: Q4 interest, Q6 algebra, Q12 profit & loss, Q15 integers, Q17 and Q22 ratio. Fixing the template in those slots would remove most of the OUT questions.
