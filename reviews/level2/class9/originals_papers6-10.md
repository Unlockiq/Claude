**09-Q30 detail:** cylinder π·4·6 = 24π, cone (1/3)π·4·3 = 4π, total 28π, which is option **B**. The AK's own worked solution ends "Total = 24π + 4π = 28π cm³" but its letter column says **C** (24π, the cylinder alone). This looks like an option-shuffle bug: on the rendered page the Q30 option labels are off-colour (A is orange) and C/D are misaligned. The wording is also weak: "What is the total volume of the **cone**" for an object that is a cylinder plus a cone. It should say "of the container" or "of the solid".
