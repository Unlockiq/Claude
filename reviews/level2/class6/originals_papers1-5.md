# Review — original AMO L2 "Extra Mock" Class 6, Tests 01–05

Source: `c6/clean/AMO_L2_Extra_Mock_Class6_Test0{1..5}_QP.pdf` + `_AK.pdf`. All 30 questions in each paper were solved independently, and the answers were then compared with the key. Figures were checked on 110-dpi renders. The papers contain only a few figures: P1 Q10 and Q25, and Q4 in P2, P3 and P5. All figures are vector drawings or a single bar-graph image.
(Process note: the answer letters for keys 4 and 5 appeared in one terminal output alongside key 3, before P4 and P5 had been solved. P4 and P5 were still worked through in full afterwards, and every working was checked independently.)

Legend: syllabus tags. **PL** = profit & loss/discount/partnership. **SI** = simple interest. **π** = circle/cylinder/π. **√** = needs a square root or a surd. **EQ** = solving a linear equation (beyond the simplest) or simultaneous equations. **ID** = algebraic identity (x+1/x)², (a+b)² and similar (C7/C8). **PAR** = parallel lines/transversal. **STAT** = mean/median/mode/range (C7). **AP** = formal arithmetic progression and sum formula (beyond "number patterns"). **WORK** = time & work/pipes (not in C6 list). **RAT** = rational numbers. **EXP** = powers/cubes as exponent work. **Rs** = uses "Rs." instead of ₹.
Difficulty: E/M/H, where H means 3+ steps or real insight.
