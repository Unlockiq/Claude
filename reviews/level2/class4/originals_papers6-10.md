# Review: original AMO Class 4 Level-2 practice papers 6–10 (reviewer B)

Sources: `c4/clean/Test06–10_QP.pdf` (rendered in `c4/review_b/`), keys `c4/in/Test06–10_AK.pdf`. I solved each question before reading the key. I checked every figure (Q10 and Q26 in each paper are the only questions with figures) at 200–400 dpi.

## Summary

**Wrong keys: 0 / 150.** Every key letter matches my own answer.

**Broken or ambiguous questions: 1 firm, 2 minor**
- **P6 Q4** (144, 196, 225, 240): two answers can be defended. 240 is the only non-square (the key's answer, D), but 225 is the only odd number (C). The question gives no hint that would rule one out.
- P6 Q26 (minor, figure): the 65° label sits in the upper-right angle at the top intersection, and that angle is drawn as about 130°, which is obtuse. Since x is the matching corresponding angle, the key (65°) is consistent with the labels. A student who goes by the drawing, though, will be tempted by 115° (B).
- P10 Q10 (minor, figure): "6 cm" is printed in the centre of the inner square instead of along one side. It is readable as the side length.

**Solution texts with errors or out-of-syllabus methods** (all key letters still correct)
- P6 Q13: says "10:45 PM + 4:30 = 15:15 → 3:15 AM". The 15:15 is wrong in 24-hour terms; it should be 27:15, or 22:45 + 4:30.
- P7 Q10: the hint invokes "Pythagoras", though all three sides are given. P8 Q2: uses √132. P9 Q3 and P9 Q22: use HCF. P9 Q23: compares with 0.125, 0.167 and 0.333 (3-place decimals). P7 Q1: uses "ceil()".
