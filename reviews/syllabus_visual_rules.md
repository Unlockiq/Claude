# Visual Standards check: Rule 9 (number lines) and Rule 12(c) (greyscale)

Scope: AMO Level 1 Practice Papers 1-5, Class 1 and Class 2 (10 papers). Page images were checked at 110 dpi.

## Rule 9: Number lines (violations and borderline cases)

Every number line seen is horizontal, has arrows on both ends and has equal tick spacing. The failures are all about tick labelling.

| Class | Paper | Q | Finding | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Q7 | Line 40-50 with ticks every 1. Only 40, 45 and 50 are numbered. Four ticks carry lettered dots (A=43, B=46, C=47, D=49). Three more ticks (41, 42, 44) have no number at all. That makes 7 unnumbered ticks, against a rule that allows ONE. | Number every tick 40-50, then put the letters A-D above the dots. Or recast the question as "Which number is at the dot?" with a single unnumbered tick at 47. |
| 1 | 2 | Q11 | Line 30-40 with ticks every 1. Only 30, 35 and 40 are numbered. The dot (37) is unnumbered, as are 8 other ticks (31-34, 36, 38, 39). | Number every tick except 37. |
| 2 | 1 | Q8 | Line 300-400 with ticks every 10. Two ticks (330 and 390) are unnumbered and each carries an unlettered dot. The question asks about 360, which IS numbered. So two ticks are blank, and neither is the tick the question asks about. | Number 330 and 390 (or letter the dots P and Q) and blank 360 with a "?". Or keep the dots blank and remove the 360 label, which still leaves two blanks, so the better fix is to label both dots. |
| 2 | 2 | Q3 | Line 400-500 with ticks every 10. Four ticks are lettered and unnumbered: A=430, B=460, C=470, D=490. The other ticks are numbered. That is 4 non-numbered ticks against a rule that allows ONE. | Number 430, 460 and 490 and keep only one blank or lettered tick at 470 ("Which number is at the dot?"). | 2 | 5 | Q41 | Line 200-400 with ticks every 10, but only every 20 is numbered. Ten ticks (210, 230 … 390) are unnumbered. F (200) and A (400) sit on numbered ticks. **Number-line questions that fully comply:** C1 P1 Q36; C1 P3 Q17; C2 P1 Q3; C2 P3 Q5, Q9, Q14; C2 P4 Q3, Q8 (A and B sit on numbered ticks); C2 P5 Q2 (only the start tick 702 is blank), Q8. (C1 P4 and C1 P5 have no number lines. The "jumps", "hops", "track" and "row" wording elsewhere refers to number cards, tiles or game tracks, not number lines.)

## Rule 12(c): Greyscale readability (violations and borderline cases)

No question depends on colour alone. Every colour-coded figure is also told apart by shape, printed label, hatching, a star or position. In text-only colour questions (C1 P1 Q21 bows, C1 P2 Q21 beads, C2 P1 Q16 apples, C2 P1 Q19 albums) the colour is just a name and the counts are given in words. Patterns that use colour (C1 P1 Q38, Q42; C1 P2 Q35, Q38; C1 P4 Q39, Q42; C1 P5 Q37, Q40, Q42; C2 P5 Q43) all change shape as well. Shaded cells use diagonal hatching (C1 P2 Q36, C1 P4 Q28, C2 P3 Q26). C1 P1 Q43 marks its months with stars, and C2 P4 Q45 prints the coin value on each coin.

| Class | Paper | Q | Finding | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | All 10 | Cover | The "For parents and teachers" box says "Print or view this paper in colour. Pictures are part of the questions." This conflicts with 12(c): it tells users that colour is needed, and no question actually needs it. | Reword to "Pictures are part of the questions. The paper can be printed in colour or in black and white." |
| 1 | 4 | Q28 (borderline, wording) | The text says "One more box must be coloured", but the filled boxes are grey with hatching. The figure is fine in greyscale, but the word "coloured" does not match what the child sees. | Change to "One more box must be shaded". |
| 2 | 3 | Q37 (borderline) | The game track alternates pastel green and yellow squares, which are almost the same in greyscale. The squares have black borders, so the count of 12 is still readable. | Optional: make the outlines thicker or hatch alternate squares. No change is needed for correctness. |
